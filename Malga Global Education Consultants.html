Our strategic focus: Africa, Asia and Europe

We focus on one route and serve it well. Every adviser, process and partner relationship at Malga is built around students moving from Africa to universities in Asia and Europe.

Where our students start: Africa

  • Scholarship and funding searches matched to your profile
  • Help verifying and presenting academic transcripts
  • Visa preparation that reflects the requirements in your country
  • Regional support from our base in Kenya

Where they study: Asia and Europe

  • Undergraduate, postgraduate and pathway programmes
  • Guidance on English-language tests and score targets
  • Financial documents prepared to embassy standards
  • Support for families who are comparing several destinations

Applying from outside Africa? Write to us, and we will tell you honestly whether we can help.
